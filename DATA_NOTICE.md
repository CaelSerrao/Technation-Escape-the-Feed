# Data and code provenance

This research uses GroupLens MovieLens 32M. The raw dataset is downloaded from its official source and is not included in this repository. Saved models, recommendation indices and user-level CSVs are transformations of that dataset and remain subject to the original MovieLens conditions reproduced in [docs/MovieLens_README.txt](docs/MovieLens_README.txt). The MovieLens user IDs are the dataset's pseudonymous IDs; no real names or demographics are included. GroupLens and the University of Minnesota do not endorse this project.

Dataset citation: F. Maxwell Harper and Joseph A. Konstan. 2015. The MovieLens Datasets: History and Context. ACM TiiS 5(4), Article 19. https://doi.org/10.1145/2827872

The analysis scripts supplied by the team were adapted only for portable input/output paths. The graphic builder and independent verification script were added during review. Analysis and report preparation were AI-assisted. No additional open-source code license is assigned here; the team can choose one after confirming ownership and agreement.
